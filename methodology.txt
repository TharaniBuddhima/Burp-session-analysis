Methodology

1. Started DVWA and Burp Suite.
2. Configured browser traffic through Burp Proxy.
3. Captured the login request using Burp Suite Intercept.
4. Analyzed authentication parameters in the request.
5. Reviewed the login response.
6. Identified and analyzed the PHPSESSID session cookie.
7. Examined cookie security attributes including HttpOnly and SameSite.
8. Captured and reviewed the logout request.
9. Analyzed the logout response.
10. Verified session termination after logout.
11. Attempted to access protected resources after logout.
12. Documented findings and observations.
